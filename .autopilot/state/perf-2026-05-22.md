**Fair matched-thread (libdeflate): ours wins -t1 2.1×, -t4 1.91×, -t8 1.57×, -t16 1.10×.**
**Scaling (ours, libdeflate): t1→t16 = 6.06× (sub-linear, plateaus ~t8).**
libdeflate gains shrink with thread count (1.23× at t1 → ~1.0× at t16) → the
high-thread bottleneck is NOT inflate; it is noodles MultithreadedReader's
single serial block-reader thread (+ main-thread parse). Next lever to push
the plateau: parallel block prefetch / multiple readers, not faster inflate.

- 68% deflate_decompress_bmi2 (libdeflate inflate, on worker threads) — the
  core work, parallelized. 13% memcpy (noodles block-data copy). ~19% other.
- Conclusion (CORRECTS earlier "serial reader thread" guess): inflate-bound.
  With libdeflate our inflate == samtools' codec. The t8→t16 plateau (ours
  1.07× vs samtools 1.53×) is memory bandwidth + noodles MultithreadedReader
  feeding/copy overhead at high worker counts, not a fixable serial stage.
- We still win at every matched thread count. Further high-thread gains need
  cutting the 13% memcpy (noodles-internal) or a custom lower-copy reader —
